For industry and financial institutions

Compliance resources and certain required disclosures

If you work in the financial services industry, a regulation section probably directed you to this page to find the most recent version of a disclosure. For additional regulatory help, explore our other compliance resources.

Your Home Loan Toolkit is the special information booklet required by Regulation X, 12 CFR 1024.6 and Regulation Z, 12 CFR 1026.19(g). Providing the booklet to your customers helps them understand the home buying process and the nature and costs of real estate settlement services. You are required to provide the toolkit to consumers after application, and all market participants are encouraged to provide the toolkit to consumers as early in the home or mortgage shopping process as possible.

You can begin using the revised toolkit immediately, use an earlier version until your existing supplies are exhausted, or use a suitable substitute to comply with the requirement. When reprinting, use the most recent version. Print-ready files are posted below, as well as web-enabled files for electronic delivery.

The CHARM booklet outlines the features and risks of adjustable rate mortgages. You are required under 12 CFR 1026.19(b)(1) to deliver this booklet to consumers who apply for an ARM.

You can use an earlier version until your existing supplies are exhausted, begin using the revised booklet immediately, or use a suitable substitute to comply with the requirement. When reprinting, use the most recent version. Print-ready files are posted below, as well as web-enabled files for electronic delivery.

The brochure, “What You Should Know About Home Equity Lines of Credit,” covers the features and risks of HELOCs. You are required under 12 CFR 1026.40(e) to provide this brochure to consumers who apply for a HELOC.

You can use an earlier version until your existing supplies are exhausted, begin using the revised brochure immediately, or use a suitable substitute to comply with the requirement. When reprinting, use the most recent version. Print-ready files are posted below, as well as web-enabled files for electronic delivery.
