Picturing what you’re saving for

Updated Jun 01, 2026

Students draw pictures of a savings goal and discuss the importance of saving money over time.

Big idea

Saving money can help us get the things we want in life.

Essential questions

  • Why is it a good idea to save money?
  • What do I want to save money for?

Objectives

  • Understand the importance of having a goal for saving money
  • Practice creating a plan for reaching a savings goal

What students will do

  • Draw a picture of something they’d like to save money for.
  • Plan how they’ll save money for what they’ve drawn.

Key information for this activity

Building block

(What are the building blocks?)

  • Executive function
  • Financial habits and norms

Topic

Save and invest (Saving for long-term goals, Saving for short-term goals)

School subject

English or language arts, Fine arts and performing arts, Math, Social studies or history

Audience

Grade level: Elementary school (K-1), Elementary school (2-3), Elementary school (4-5)

Age range: 5-7, 7-9, 9-11

Student characteristics: English language learners, Special education, Low income, Rural, Urban

Activity characteristics

Activity type: Individual

Teaching strategy: Personalized instruction

Bloom’s Taxonomy level: Understand, Apply, Create

Activity duration: 45-60 minutes

National standards

(What are the national standards? )

Standards: I. Earning income, III. Saving
